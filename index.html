FAQs

Questions, answered.

Will checking eligibility affect my credit score?

No. Filling this form does not pull your credit report. A credit enquiry happens only when your formal application is processed by the lending partner, and that is done with your consent.

How fast can I get the money?

Approval decisions are quick once your documents are in. After verification and agreement signing, the amount is disbursed directly to your bank account. Uploading clear documents and answering your expert's call speeds things up.

Do I need a guarantor or collateral?

No. This is an unsecured personal loan, so you don't need to pledge anything or find a guarantor.

Can self-employed people apply?

Yes. Self-employed individuals and business owners can apply, subject to income and eligibility criteria.

Can I close the loan early?

Yes. Prepayment from your own funds carries no charge. Prepayment through a balance transfer to another lender attracts 4% of the prepaid amount plus taxes.

Why can an application be rejected?

Common reasons include not meeting eligibility criteria, unstable or insufficient income, high existing EMIs, a weak credit profile, or mismatched information. Your expert will tell you what can be improved.

Is my data safe?

Your details travel over an encrypted connection and are used only to process your loan request with our lending partner.
